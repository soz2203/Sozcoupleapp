Privacy Policy — Soz

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

Soz is a private mobile app designed for two bonded partners to share messages, photos, moods, and calls. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how it's protected.

Information We Collect

  • An anonymous account identifier created automatically when you open the app (no email or phone number required to use Soz)
  • If you choose to link a Google account for backup: your name, email, and profile photo
  • Messages, photos, and voice notes you exchange with your partner
  • Mood selections, Tease Room activity, daily challenges, goals, and timeline events
  • Call metadata for audio/video calls (call audio/video itself is end-to-end encrypted peer-to-peer and is never stored)
  • A device push-notification token, used to deliver messages from your partner
  • Subscription/purchase status if you buy Soz Premium, verified through Google Play Billing
  • Basic crash and diagnostic reports (via Firebase Crashlytics) to help us fix bugs
  • Advertising identifiers and interaction data if you view ads (see "Advertising" below)

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide real-time messaging, calls, and mood sync between bonded partners
  • To display your shared vault of photos and voice notes
  • To deliver push notifications when your partner sends a message or changes their mood
  • To maintain your bonded connection through your private 6-digit Love PIN
  • To process and verify Premium subscription purchases
  • To diagnose and fix crashes or errors
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Ever.

End-to-End Encryption

Chat messages, vault media, and Partner's Wall posts are end-to-end encrypted: only you and your bonded partner hold the keys to decrypt them, and the keys live only on your devices. If you enable the optional Google Backup link, your decryption key is also backed up to your private account record so you don't lose access to old messages after reinstalling — turning this on trades a small amount of that guarantee for convenience, and it's entirely your choice.

Data Storage

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore database) and Cloudinary (photo/video hosting). Shared data is stored under a private, per-couple record accessible only to you and your bonded partner, enforced by server-side security rules.

Data Shared With Your Partner

The following is visible to your bonded partner only:

  • Your display name and profile photo
  • Your current mood
  • Messages, photos, and voice notes you send
  • Your activity in shared features (Tease Room, goals, challenges, timeline)

Advertising

Soz shows ads via Google AdMob to free users. AdMob may use advertising identifiers to show relevant ads, subject to the consent choice you make when prompted (required in the EU/UK/certain US states, per Google's User Messaging Platform). You can review or change your ad privacy choices at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy Options. Soz Premium removes ads entirely.

Permissions

Soz requests the following device permissions, only when needed:

  • Camera — to take photos/videos to share
  • Microphone — to record voice notes and for audio/video calls
  • Photo/Video Library — to pick and save media
  • Push Notifications — to notify you of partner activity

All permissions can be revoked at any time in your device settings.

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account and bond exist. Deleting your account permanently removes your personal information, messages, and media from our servers. Breaking up with your partner (in-app) also permanently ends shared access to that data.

Children's Privacy

Soz is intended for adults only and is not directed to anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

We use server-side security rules that restrict all shared data to the two bonded partners only, end-to-end encryption for message/media content, and industry-standard authentication. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information using currently accepted industry standards.

Third-Party Services

Soz relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (data storage, authentication, crash reporting, push notifications)
  • Cloudinary (photo/video hosting)
  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Google Play Billing (subscription purchases)
  • Cloudflare Workers (backend processing for billing, media deletion, and calls)

Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Delete your account and all associated data (Settings → Delete Account)
  • Change your ad consent choices at any time
  • Turn off notifications at any time
  • Contact us with privacy concerns
