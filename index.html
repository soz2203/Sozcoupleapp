Soz — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

Soz is a private mobile application designed exclusively for two bonded partners. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data responsibly.

Information We Collect

  • Google account information (name, email, profile photo) obtained through Google Sign-In
  • Messages and media (photos, videos, voice notes) exchanged between you and your partner
  • Mood selections, tease/challenge activity, and heart session activity
  • Device push notification token (to send you messages from your partner)
  • Device/advertising identifiers, collected by our advertising partner (Google AdMob) if you have not opted out — see "Advertising" below
  • Purchase records for optional Premium subscriptions, processed by Google Play Billing
  • App usage and crash/error data to improve performance and stability

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide real-time messaging, calling, and mood sync between bonded partners
  • To display your shared vault of photos, videos, and voice notes
  • To deliver push notifications when your partner sends you a message or changes their mood
  • To maintain your bonded connection through our secure 6-digit Love PIN system
  • To show ads (free tier only) and to process optional Premium purchases
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Ever.

End-to-End Encryption

Chat messages and Vault media are end-to-end encrypted on your device before they ever leave it, using a private key that is generated on your device and never transmitted to us. This means we cannot read your messages or view your Vault photos and videos, even if asked to — we simply don't hold the keys. Because the key lives only on your device, it does not automatically survive an uninstall/reinstall unless you've linked Google Backup for your account (Settings → Backup & Restore) beforehand.

Data Storage

Your account, profile, and app data are stored using Google Firebase (Firestore database and Firebase Authentication). Photos, videos, and voice notes are stored using Cloudinary, a third-party media hosting service — media is encrypted before upload where end-to-end encryption applies (see above). Call connection setup and some background processing run on Cloudflare Workers. Your shared data is scoped to your private partner-pair record, accessible only to you and your bonded partner.

Data Shared with Your Partner

The following information is visible to your bonded partner only:

  • Your display name and profile photo
  • Your current mood
  • Messages, photos, videos, and voice notes you send in chat
  • Your heart session and tease/challenge activity
  • Your push notification token (used only to notify you)

Advertising

Free-tier use of Soz includes ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. Before any ad is requested, we run Google's consent flow, which shows a consent/options form where required by your region (including the EEA, UK, and regulated US states). You can review or change your ad privacy choices at any time via Settings → Ad Privacy Options. Premium subscribers do not see ads.

In-App Purchases

Soz offers an optional Premium subscription, purchased and processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We receive purchase confirmation (needed to unlock Premium features) but never your payment card details — those are handled solely by Google Play.

Permissions

Soz requests the following device permissions:

  • Camera — to take photos to share in chat and for video calls
  • Microphone — to record voice notes and for voice/video calls
  • Photo Library — to pick photos/videos and save received media
  • Push Notifications — to notify you of partner activity

All permissions are requested only when needed and can be revoked at any time through your device settings.

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we permanently delete all your personal information, messages, and media from our servers. Your partner's data remains unaffected by your account deletion.

Delete Your Account

To delete your Soz account, open the app and go to Settings → Delete Account, then confirm.

This permanently deletes your profile, chat history, Vault media (photos, videos, voice notes), and mood/activity data from our servers — including removal from Google Firebase and Cloudinary. Your partner's own account and data are not affected. This action cannot be undone.

If you're unable to access the app to complete this yourself, email us at sozcoupleapp@gmail.com from the address linked to your account and we'll process the deletion for you.

Children's Privacy

Soz is intended for adults only and is not directed to anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including Firebase Security Rules that restrict data access to authenticated users only, so that only the two bonded partners can access their shared data. Chat and Vault content is additionally end-to-end encrypted (see above).

Third-Party Services

Soz uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase (data storage and authentication)
  • Google Sign-In (authentication)
  • Cloudinary (photo/video/voice-note hosting)
  • Cloudflare Workers (call connectivity and background processing)
  • Google AdMob (advertising, free tier only)
  • Google Play Billing (Premium subscription purchases)
  • Expo Push Notifications (notification delivery)

These services have their own privacy policies which govern their use of your data.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you (note: end-to-end encrypted chat/Vault content is not something we ourselves can access or view)
  • Delete your account and all associated data (via Settings → Delete Account)
  • Withdraw consent for notifications or ad personalization at any time
  • Contact us with privacy concerns

California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know what personal information we collect and the right to opt out of the "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Ad personalization can be limited or turned off at any time via Settings → Ad Privacy Options. We do not sell personal information for money.

International Users

Soz is operated from India, and the third-party services we use (Google Firebase, Cloudinary, Cloudflare) process data on servers that may be located in other countries, including the United States. By using Soz, you consent to your information being processed in these locations.
