Soz — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Soz is a private mobile application designed exclusively for two bonded partners. This policy explains what information Soz collects, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it. By using Soz you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

  • Profile: your display name, optional profile photo, optional partner nickname and anniversary date, and your chosen app language. Soz does not require an email address, phone number, or password — your account is identified by a randomly generated account ID
  • Content you create: messages, photos, videos, and voice notes exchanged with your partner; mood selections; hearts, tease/challenge, game, goal, and wall activity; timeline events
  • Call information: when you place or receive an audio or video call, we process call connection details (who called whom, when, duration, and whether it was answered, missed, or declined)
  • Device information: a device identifier (see Device Identifiers & Diagnostics), your push notification token, and device model and OS version
  • Crash and diagnostic data: error reports and crash logs used to fix bugs and improve stability
  • Purchase information: subscription status and a purchase token used to verify your Premium subscription; we never receive your payment card details
  • Referral information: the referral code you enter, if any

We do not collect your precise or approximate location, your contacts, or your SMS or call logs.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide real-time messaging, mood sync, shared vault, games, and calls between bonded partners
  • To deliver push notifications and incoming-call alerts
  • To keep your bond secure and let you reconnect after reinstalling the app or switching phones
  • To verify Premium purchases and referral rewards
  • To detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and errors
  • To prevent abuse and enforce our Terms of Service
  • We do not sell your personal data. We do not show ads, and we do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

Audio & Video Calls

Calls between you and your partner are delivered in real time over the internet through our call infrastructure so that your audio and video can reach your partner. Soz does not record your calls and does not store call audio or video. We keep only call connection details (who, when, duration, and outcome) so that your call history and missed-call notifications work. Calls use your camera and microphone only while a call is in progress.

Encryption

Chat messages and Vault media are protected in transit by standard HTTPS/TLS, and encrypted at rest using a random secret key generated uniquely for your couple when you bond. This is NOT end-to-end encryption: your couple's key is stored on our servers, not only on your devices, so it is not derived from anything only you and your partner know or hold. In practice this means: no other user, and nobody reading a database export or backup, can read your chat or Vault content without your couple's specific key — but Soz's own backend, operating with full database administrative access, technically retains the ability to access it, as does anyone who compromises that administrative access or compels its disclosure. Access in normal use is restricted by both this per-couple encryption and account-level security rules to the two bonded partners. If you reinstall the app or switch phones and reconnect using your Love PIN, your account and bond reconnect immediately, but your chat and Vault content stays locked on that new device until your partner approves it (or, if you skip that step, until you request it again and they approve). This partner-approval step is an access control enforced by our servers, not just an on-screen prompt.

Data Storage

Your account, profile, and app data are stored in cloud databases operated by our service providers. Photos, videos, and voice notes are stored with a media hosting service as opaque encrypted ciphertext (see Encryption above). Some background processing runs on a serverless computing platform. Your shared data is scoped to your private partner-pair record and restricted by access-control rules to you and your bonded partner. See Service Providers below.

Data Shared with Your Partner

The following information is visible to your bonded partner only:

  • Your display name and profile photo
  • Your current mood
  • Messages, photos, videos, and voice notes you send in chat
  • Your heart session, tease/challenge, game, and goal activity
  • Your call history with them
  • Your push notification token (used only to notify you)
  • A pending device-restore request if you reinstall the app or switch phones, so they can approve your new device — including if you choose to skip that request, so they know your chat and Vault history is intentionally still locked rather than missing

Device Identifiers & Diagnostics

To protect your bond, Soz uses your device's app-scoped Android ID as a device identifier. It is stored with your account ID so that, after an uninstall and reinstall, we can recognise that the device already belongs to a bonded account and prevent a partner from being silently left behind. It is used for this security purpose only, never for advertising. Crash logs and diagnostic data (error traces, device model, OS version, app version, and your account ID) are collected automatically to fix bugs and are not linked to your name or email.

In-App Purchases

Soz offers an optional Premium subscription, purchased and processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We receive purchase confirmation (needed to unlock Premium features) and a purchase token that we verify with Google, but never your payment card details — those are handled solely by Google Play.

Referral Program

If you choose to enter a friend's referral code, we store which code you entered and a link between your account and the account that referred you. This is used solely to verify eligibility for the referral reward and to show the referring user, in their own Settings → Invite & Earn, who has used their code. This information is not shared with third parties and is deleted along with the rest of your account data if you delete your account.

Permissions

Soz requests the following device permissions:

  • Camera — to take photos and to send video during video calls
  • Microphone — to record voice notes and to speak during calls
  • Photos, Videos & Audio (media library) — to pick media you want to share and to save received media
  • Notifications — to notify you of partner activity and incoming calls
  • Display over other apps — to show a floating call window while you use other apps
  • Battery optimization exemption — so incoming calls can ring reliably when the app is in the background
  • Foreground service (camera/microphone) — to keep an ongoing call active
  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth — to route call audio to your headset
  • Internet, network state, vibration, wake lock, and audio settings — for core app operation

Permissions are requested only when needed and can be revoked at any time in your device settings. Some features will not work without the related permission.

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we permanently delete your profile and personal information from our servers — see "Deleting Your Account" below for how shared chat and vault content is handled. Call connection details and crash logs are kept only for as long as needed for the purposes above and are then automatically removed. Your partner's data remains unaffected by your account deletion.

Children's Privacy

Soz is intended for adults only and is not directed to anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards: HTTPS/TLS for all data in transit, account-level access-control rules so that only the two bonded partners can access their shared data in normal use, per-couple encryption at rest for chat and Vault content (see Encryption above), and an optional app lock PIN that is stored only on your device. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Service Providers

Soz relies on trusted service providers that operate on our behalf and only to run the app, for: account and database hosting, media storage, real-time call delivery, push notification delivery, crash diagnostics, purchase verification (Google Play), and background processing. They process your data only as needed to provide these services to us and are bound by their own security and privacy obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, do not share it with advertisers, and do not include advertising or marketing trackers in the app.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct your profile information in the app
  • Delete your account and personal data — via Settings → Delete My Account & Data in the app, which also ends your current bond, or by emailing us if you no longer have the app installed
  • Withdraw consent for notifications, camera, microphone, and other permissions at any time in your device settings
  • Contact us with privacy concerns or complaints

Deleting Your Account

Settings → Delete My Account & Data permanently removes your profile and personal data from our servers, and ends your current bond so your partner is unaffected and free to reconnect with someone else. Shared chat and vault content becomes inaccessible to both partners once the bond ends, consistent with how ending a bond already works, though it is not necessarily erased from our servers immediately. If you no longer have the app installed, email sozcoupleapp@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Account" and we will process your request within 7 business days.

California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know what personal information we collect, and to request deletion. We do not sell or share personal information for money or for cross-context behavioral advertising.

International Users

Soz is operated from India, and the service providers we use process data on servers that may be located in other countries, including the United States. By using Soz, you consent to your information being processed in these locations.
