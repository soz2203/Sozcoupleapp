Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Soz is a couples app. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and who receives it.

Information We Collect

  • Account: an account identifier, your Love PIN, and your referral code. When you sign in with Google, we also receive your Google account's name and email address, and use them to recognise you when you reinstall Soz or switch phones. We never receive your Google password.
  • Profile: display name, profile photo, and mood.
  • Your content: chat messages, photos, videos, voice notes, timeline entries, goals, and game and challenge activity.
  • Calls: call connection details, processed to connect your calls.
  • Purchases: purchase confirmation and token from Google Play. We never receive your payment card details.
  • Device and diagnostics: a device identifier, a notification token, and crash reports.

How We Use It

We use this information to run Soz: to connect you with your partner, store and deliver your content, send notifications, unlock Premium, fix crashes, and keep the app secure. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who Receives It

  • Your partner: the profile information and content you share with them in the app.
  • Service providers: Google (sign-in and our Firebase back end) and other companies that host our systems, store media, carry calls and notifications, process purchases, and report crashes. They process data only on our behalf.
  • Reports: if you report your partner, we keep your account ID, theirs, the reason, and the time, so we can review it.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information where the law requires it.

Encryption

Chat and media are encrypted in transit and at rest. The encryption keys are held on our servers, so Soz is not end-to-end encrypted.

Retention and Deletion

We keep your information while your account exists. If you reinstall Soz or switch phones, you sign in with the same Google account, and the bond and its content come back to the new phone only after your partner approves. Ending a bond, or deleting your account, removes the shared chat, media, and related content for both partners from our servers, and from both phones when each phone next connects. Copies in backups are removed on a set schedule. Call and crash data are kept only as long as needed for the purposes described above.

How to Delete Your Account

  • In the app, go to Settings → Delete My Account & Data and confirm.
  • If you can't access the app, email sozcoupleapp@gmail.com from the email address of the Google account you used with Soz, and we'll delete your account and data.

Your Choices

You can sign out, edit your profile, save media you receive, withdraw permissions in your device settings, and delete your account at any time. You can report or block your partner in Settings → Report or block partner. Blocking ends your bond and removes shared content, as described above.

Premium

Premium belongs to the account that bought it. While you are bonded, both partners can use Premium features, so the app checks whether your account or your partner's account has Premium.

Children

Soz is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

We update this policy and change the date above.
